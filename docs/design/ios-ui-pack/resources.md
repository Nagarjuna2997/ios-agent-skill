# Official Apple iOS UI resources

Apple's [Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/) page is the source of the destination links below. Apple can update those destinations and kit versions. Links were checked on November 15, 2026 against that page; the contents of the individual Figma and Sketch files were not inspected, and access may require a Figma or Sketch account. If a direct link changes, return to Apple's source page. Do not treat a kit version as the app's deployment target.

## Apple-authored sample projects

These links go to Apple-owned sample projects with their own requirements and terms. They are not copied into this pack.

- [Landmarks: Building an app with Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/landmarks-building-an-app-with-liquid-glass) — system and custom Liquid Glass in an app.
- [Food Truck: Building a SwiftUI multiplatform app](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/food-truck-building-a-swiftui-multiplatform-app) — a larger iPhone, iPad and Mac sample.
- [SwiftUI Pathway](https://developer.apple.com/swiftui/get-started/) — Apple's current learning entry point and sample links.
